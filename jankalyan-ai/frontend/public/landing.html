How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us about yourself

    Type or speak in English, Kannada or Hindi.

  2. 2

    We check the scheme rules

    Rules run locally on the scheme catalogue. An AI model reads your description.

  3. 3

    Confirm on the official portal

    Every result links to the government site.

Good to know

  • The app does not verify documents or decide eligibility.
  • Only the department can sanction a benefit.
  • Identity and bank documents are not accepted for upload.
